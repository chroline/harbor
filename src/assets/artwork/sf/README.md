# San Francisco skyline backgrounds

Original artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool on October 20, 2026, and edited with the same tool on October 21 to include the Golden Gate Bridge in every lighting variant. The daytime composition is the reference for the three lighting edits. All four are bundled locally as optimized 1536×1024 WebP images; the interface retains its existing Fluid theme.

The Bay Bridge remains on the left, with the Golden Gate Bridge's orange suspension towers in the distant right. This is a stylized panorama rather than an exact geographic view.

The [Golden Gate revision prompts](GOLDEN_GATE_PROMPTS.md) record the current asset edits; the original generation prompts are preserved below.

These are fixed clock periods, not astronomical sunrise/sunset calculations. The background refreshes each minute and when the app regains focus or visibility. Images are decorative and do not change the selected UI theme.

## Original day prompt

Create an original full-background illustration for a desktop app: SAN FRANCISCO from the bay, viewed from the northeast looking toward the downtown waterfront, at clear late-morning daylight. Landscape 3:2 composition, ideally 2304x1536. A beautiful authentic San Francisco skyline with the distinctive tapered Salesforce Tower, pointed Transamerica Pyramid, layered Telegraph Hill and small Coit Tower, tightly packed stepped city blocks, the Embarcadero waterfront; geographically plausible city silhouette. A little distant Bay Bridge may enter from the far left edge, but the city itself is the subject. Sky occupies the top 45%, buildings between 35% and 60%, expansive calm bay water the bottom 40%. The skyline is spread across the width; strongest architectural landmarks stay within the middle 60% so they survive a narrower crop. Quiet pale cerulean sky, soft coastal haze, thin rolling fog toward distant hills, sun-warmed ivory and gray architecture, deep muted teal water, gentle rippled reflections. Art direction: exquisite editorial pixel painting with very fine intentional dithering and stippling, intricate architecture, painterly lighting, nostalgic but sophisticated; no chunky 8-bit sprites, no photorealism. Restrained natural colors, spacious and contemplative. The bottom 25% is low-contrast water suitable for an interface overlay. Edge-to-edge artwork ONLY, no text, UI, frame, logos, watermarks, foreground people, cartoon characters, boats in the center or giant sun. No lighthouse, no paper sculpture, no fantasy skyline. This is the master composition for matching dawn, sunset and night versions; make the buildings and viewpoint coherent.
