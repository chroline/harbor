# Los Angeles skyline backgrounds

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on October 21, 2026, and revised on October 27 with the user-selected composition showing the Hollywood sign and a small USC campus within a flat urban neighborhood. The artwork matches the SF collection. Day is the master composition; dawn, sunset, and night preserve its architecture and viewpoint while changing lighting. Assets are saved locally as optimized WebP files. Select this skyline in Settings → Appearance → Background city. Lighting follows the user’s local clock: dawn 05:00–07:59, day 08:00–16:59, sunset 17:00–19:59, night 20:00–04:59.

The Hollywood sign is on the left hillside; USC is a modest brick campus with a red-and-gold banner in the lower-left urban area. This is a stylized panorama rather than an exact geographic view. The [Hollywood and USC revision prompts](HOLLYWOOD_USC_PROMPTS.json) record the current edits.
